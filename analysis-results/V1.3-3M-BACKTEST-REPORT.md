## Cost sensitivity
The cost filter is re-evaluated under each cost assumption, so lower assumed costs allow more trades through; this is why lower costs do not mechanically produce a better total return.

- Base 10 bps fee each side + 5 bps exit slippage: 30 trades, -10.8883%, max DD 11.2935%, PF 0.3616.
- Lower-cost 5 bps fee each side + 2 bps exit slippage: 110 trades, 41.82% net win rate, -27.1221%, max DD 28.2038%, PF 0.5075. The looser cost gate admits many additional weak trades.
- Zero trading cost: 139 trades, 44.60% win rate, +10.3633%, max DD 10.8961%, PF 1.1432, gross expectancy +0.1151R/trade.

## Interpretation
1. V1.3 fixes the V1.2.3 problem of producing almost no signals, but signal quality is now the main issue: the base executed set is exactly break-even in R before costs.
2. The current cost filter removes many low-volatility trades, but the surviving trades still do not carry enough gross edge to pay the conservative execution budget.
3. Score 9 is the clearest weak pocket in this 3-month sample; score 10 performed best, but only has four trades, which is far too small for a threshold conclusion.
4. Shorts remain weaker than longs during a period in which BTC rose about 26.2%.
5. More trades becoming eligible when assumed costs are lower actually worsens performance, showing that the economics filter is suppressing a large pool of low-quality signals rather than merely shaving costs.
6. Three months and 30 base-case trades are not enough to validate profitability. A 12–24 month multi-regime replay should be done before changing production thresholds based on these numbers.

## Limitations
- Historical best bid/ask is unavailable, so the limit price is proxied by the signal 5m close; live spread and the 0.3 x 1H ATR chase check are not reconstructed.
- Partial fills cannot be inferred from candle OHLC; fills are modeled as full fill or no fill.
- Funding is excluded.
- The absolute daily-equity-loss cap is excluded because it depends on the user's local USDT configuration; the China natural-day 3-consecutive-loss guard is included.
- Same-bar intrabar path is unknown; if both SL and TP are present, SL is counted first.
- Backtest performance is not a guarantee of future or live performance.
